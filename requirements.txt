NEXORA — FEATURE REQUIREMENTS

PURPOSE
Build a future-career matching and reskilling platform that assesses a learner's
skills, incorporates updateable AI and labour-market signals, recommends suitable
future roles, and presents actionable learning paths and uncertainty scenarios.

============================================================
A. MUST-HAVE FEATURES (REQUIRED)
============================================================

FR-01: SKILLS PROFILE INTAKE
- Allow users to provide their current skills and potential using at least one
  of these methods: CV/resume upload, form, or guided questionnaire.
- Extract or collect skills and display an editable skills profile.
- Use the skills profile as input to role recommendations and gap analysis.
Acceptance: A user can submit skills information, review their captured skills,
  and use it to generate recommendations.

FR-02: AI-TREND SIGNAL LAYER
- Integrate at least ONE updateable EXTERNAL source of AI and job-market trends.
  Examples: public job-posting datasets, news/research feeds, open datasets.
- Store or retrieve source information and its update/publication date.
- Show the source name/link and date alongside trend insights.
- A hard-coded list of jobs alone does NOT meet this requirement.
Acceptance: The application demonstrably uses one refreshable external source,
  and users can see the source and relevant date.

FR-03: FUTURE-ROLE MATCHING
- Recommend and RANK suitable future career roles based on the user's profile
  and AI/job-market trend signals.
- Show an EXPLAINABLE match score for each recommended role.
- Show the SKILLS GAP for each role (skills already present vs. skills missing
  or needing improvement).
Acceptance: The user sees a ranked role list, a comprehensible score/explanation,
  and skill gaps for each role.

FR-04: PERSONALISED RESKILLING PATHWAY
- Generate a PERSONALISED, ORDERED learning plan for the selected target role.
- Include an estimated duration/time for each learning step.
- Recommend FREE or LOW-COST resources, such as online courses and HRD Corp
  claimable programmes (where applicable).
- Link learning steps to the user's specific skills gaps.
Acceptance: A user can see what to learn first, why, how long each step should
  take, and where to learn it affordably.

FR-05: SDG MAPPING
- Tag EACH recommended role and skill to at least one United Nations
  Sustainable Development Goal (SDG).
- Explain how the selected user's career path and learning goals contribute
  to those SDGs.
Acceptance: A user can view SDG tags and a clear explanation for both roles
  and skills/pathway contributions.

FR-06: HANDLING UNCERTAINTY
- Present MORE THAN ONE plausible future scenario (for example, moderate AI
  adoption versus fast AI disruption).
- Show how the scenario may affect roles, demand, matching, or learning priorities.
- Clearly label forecasts, risk levels and projections as ESTIMATES, not facts
  or guarantees; avoid presenting a single certain prediction.
Acceptance: A user can compare at least two scenarios and sees a visible
  uncertainty/estimates disclaimer.

============================================================
B. GOOD-TO-HAVE FEATURES (OPTIONAL)
Not separately required or scored; may strengthen Innovation & Creativity
or Problem–Solution Fit.
============================================================

OPT-01: EMPLOYER / HR VIEW
- Provide workforce-level skills-gap heatmaps and reskilling recommendations
  for a team or organisation.

OPT-02: AUTOMATIC RE-PLANNING
- Refresh or adjust role recommendations and learning plans when new AI-trend
  or labour-market data becomes available.

OPT-03: INCLUSIVE DESIGN
- Offer multilingual support (English, Bahasa Malaysia, Chinese).
- Support a lightweight, mobile-friendly, low-bandwidth experience.

OPT-04: LEARNER MOTIVATION
- Track learning progress and completion.
- Support skill badges or similar recognition/incentives.

OPT-05: FAIRNESS CHECKS
- Review recommendations for potential discrimination or biased outcomes
  related to age, gender, or background.
- Avoid basing suitability rankings on protected characteristics.

OPT-06: SDG CONTRIBUTION VIEW
- Aggregate and visualise SDG contributions for an individual, company,
  or region.
